# Single-Stage-Gear-Reducer-Design-and-Calculation
Analytical engineering calculations (MS Excel), 3D CAD assembly, motion study, and technical documentation of a single-stage cylindrical gear reducer modeled in SOLIDWORKS.

## Project Overview

Independent mechanical engineering project focused on the design and 3D CAD modeling of a single-stage gear reducer, including mechanical component design, engineering calculations, and parametric calculation automation.

## Engineering Activities

### Mechanical Design and Engineering Calculations
- Gear geometry and dimensional calculations
- Shaft design and preliminary dimensioning
- Bearing selection and arrangement
- Design of shaft–gear connections using parallel keys
- Dimensional definition of mechanical components

### CAD Modeling – SolidWorks
- 3D modeling of individual mechanical components
- Complete assembly modeling and component integration
- Design of stepped shafts and gear mounting features
- Bearing and keyway integration
- Assembly visualization and component positioning

### Engineering Calculation Automation – Microsoft Excel
- Development of an Excel-based engineering calculation spreadsheet
- Parametric calculation automation
- Automatic updating of calculated dimensions based on input parameters
- Organization of engineering calculations into a structured spreadsheet

## Software
- SolidWorks – 3D CAD modeling and assembly design
- Microsoft Excel – engineering calculations and parametric spreadsheet development

## Project Documentation
- 3D CAD models
- Gear reducer assembly
- Selected mechanical component models
- Engineering calculation spreadsheet
- Assembly visualizations

## Project Status
CAD modeling and preliminary engineering calculations completed.
